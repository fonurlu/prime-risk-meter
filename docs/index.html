How the score works

Each indicator has a calm level (score 0) and a danger level (score 100); today's value is placed on that line and capped. Indicators are averaged within their pillar, and the pillars are weighted 30 / 25 / 25 / 20 into one number. The contrarian flag lights when sentiment is fearful (50+) while recession and credit signals stay calm (under 30): historically a correction rather than a recession. Markets data refresh every 15 minutes; economic data daily to monthly. Not investment advice.

Data: FRED (St. Louis Fed), Yahoo Finance, CNN Fear & Greed, AAII, ISM, ValueTrend.
